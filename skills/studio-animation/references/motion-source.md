# Motion source

Found in one game (Nov 2026), after the walks of its two-legged creatures were redone from filmed people. Dated, so re-check against the tools in use.

Choose the motion source before animating.

- For a move a person can act out, start from motion capture or a filmed person.
- Use video-to-rig or hand-keyed poses only for moves no person can act.
- Let the captured motion drive the body and legs, and pull the striking limb onto the gameplay contact point during the reach.
- A clip sped up to fit gameplay timing needs its follow-through added back as a cosmetic tail.

Every recorded move still passes the [move check](move-check.md) before a review.
