# Sources

Primary frameworks:

- Hunicke, LeBlanc and Zubek, "MDA: A Formal Approach to Game Design and Game Research" (2004): https://users.cs.northwestern.edu/~hunicke/pubs/MDA.pdf. The eight kinds of fun and the mechanics, dynamics, aesthetics model.
- Riot Games, URF Academy, "Game Feeling & 8 Kinds of Fun": https://www.riotgames.com/en/urf-academy/fun-and-feeling. Riot's own teaching of the taxonomy.
- Riot Games, "Player Dynamics design: looking behind the curtain": https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/player-dynamics-design-looking-behind-the-curtain. Designing social structures from prototyping onward.
- Nicole Lazzaro, XEODesign, "4 Keys 2 Fun": hard, easy, people and serious fun.
- Walk, Görlich and Barrett, "Design, Dynamics, Experience (DDE): An Advancement of the MDA Framework for Game Design" (2017), for when the eight kinds feel too coarse.

Wyatt Cheng:

- The one-word series (YouTube Shorts, mirrored on Instagram): Cuphead https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YXb2jOoJ6ck, It Takes Two https://www.youtube.com/shorts/j_b4kZJWRRs, Beat Saber https://www.youtube.com/shorts/f7VkUSWYJPw, Vampire Survivors https://www.youtube.com/shorts/V4vPnB4Hcss, Dispatch https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YVzOcvwGtK4, Cyberpunk 2077 https://www.youtube.com/shorts/t_Zkqz0NkjU. Verdicts were taken from the videos' audio.
- GDC 2013, "Through the Grinder: Refining Diablo III's Game Systems", transcript: https://www.purediablo.com/wyatt-chengs-gdc-panel-full-video-transcript. Death readability, health globes, avoiding the grand reveal.
- Diablo III PvP retrospective (fun to play as and fun to play against): https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/diablo-3-nearly-had-league-of-legends-pvp-mode-3228215.

Respawn and mode design:

- Ubisoft, "Behind the Wall: One Life" (Siege's Support Mode): https://www.ubisoft.com/en-gb/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/4CK0Vbt3CzbYpBDTYgeDCo/behind-the-wall-series-one-life.
- Game Informer, Valorant deep dive (deliberate no-respawn, its inspirations): https://gameinformer.com/preview/2020/03/02/valorant-preview-a-deep-dive-on-the-new-hero-based-tactical-shooter-from-riot.
- Overwatch patch notes, Group Respawn (October 2023): https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2023/10/; Season 12 wave respawns for the current state.
- Kaplan on team versus individual contribution: https://www.thegamer.com/overwatch-ex-boss-regrets-team-focus/.
- Eurogamer, Apex Legends launch design (banners and rescue): https://www.eurogamer.net/articles/2019-02-04-respawn-explains-apex-legends-surprise-release-and-why-there-are-no-titans.
- Valve on Team Fortress 2's Medic problem (a vital role nobody played, fixed by re-incentivizing it): https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/in-depth-valve-on-i-team-fortress-2-i---devs-as-service-providers.

Caveats: round timers and respawn timings for shipped games are as published around October 2026 and move with patches. Cheng's series has no episode on a team shooter, co-operative shooter or arena game, so every application of the vocabulary to those genres in this skill is synthesis, marked as such. The original research behind this skill was compiled for one asymmetric team game; its project-specific findings stay in that project's records, and the invented example in [worked examples](worked-examples.md) is illustrative only.
